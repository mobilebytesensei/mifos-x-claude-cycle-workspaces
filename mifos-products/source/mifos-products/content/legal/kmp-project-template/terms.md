## 2. Description

Money Toolkit is an open-source, cross-platform app from the Mifos Initiative, built with Kotlin Multiplatform. It offers loan and EMI calculators, a loan tracker, bill reminders, and interest-rate, currency, country-macro and crypto market views on Android, iOS, macOS, desktop and the web.
