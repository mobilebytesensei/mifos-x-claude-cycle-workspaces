# Privacy Policy — KMP Project Template

**Last Updated**: March 27, 2026

## 1. Introduction

KMP Project Template ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our cross-platform task management application.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- **Task Data**: Task titles, descriptions, due dates, priority levels, and completion status
- **Profile Information**: Name and email (stored locally)
- **Preferences**: Theme, language, notification settings

### 2.2 Automatically Collected
- **Analytics Events**: App usage patterns via Firebase Analytics (Android only)
- **Device Information**: Platform type (Android, iOS, Desktop, Web)

### 2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect
- Location data
- Contact lists
- Photos or media
- Financial information
- Biometric data (passcode/biometrics stored on-device only)

## 3. How We Use Information

- Display and manage your tasks
- Remember your theme and language preferences
- Provide notification reminders for upcoming tasks
- Improve app performance via aggregated analytics

## 4. Data Storage

| Data Type | Storage | Location |
|-----------|---------|----------|
| User preferences | DataStore | On-device |
| Task data | In-memory (StorageService) | On-device |
| Sample entities | Room Database | On-device |
| Analytics | Firebase | Google Cloud |

All personal data is stored locally on your device. No data is transmitted to external servers except anonymized analytics.

## 5. Third-Party Services

| Service | Purpose | Privacy Policy |
|---------|---------|----------------|
| Firebase Analytics | Usage analytics (Android) | [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) |

## 6. Data Retention

- **On-device data**: Retained until you delete the app or clear data
- **Analytics**: Retained per Google's standard retention policies

## 7. Your Rights

You can:
- Delete all task data by clearing app storage
- Disable analytics by opting out in Settings
- Change language and theme preferences at any time
- Request deletion of analytics data via Google's tools

## 8. Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be noted in the app's release notes.

## 10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Mifos Initiative at privacy@mifos.org.
