# MifosSave — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-08 · Publisher: Mifos Initiative_

MifosSave ("the app") is open-source community-banking software that helps savings
groups (VSLAs, ROSCAs, SACCOs, SHGs and similar) keep their meeting, savings, loan
and share-out records. This policy explains what the app collects and why.

## What we collect
- **Account details** — your name and your phone number or email address, used to
  create your account and identify you within your groups.
- **Group financial records** — the savings, contributions, loans, repayments,
  fines and balances you and your group record in the app. This data belongs to
  your group and is stored to provide the app's core function.
- **Diagnostics** — anonymous crash reports and basic in-app usage events (via
  Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics) that help us fix bugs and improve reliability.

## How your data is stored and synced
MifosSave is **offline-first**. Your data is stored on your device and synced to the
**Mifos / Apache Fineract** core-banking backend operated by your organization or
group. The app does not send your group's financial records to any other third party.

## How we use your data
- To provide the app's features: meetings, savings, loans, corpus tracking and share-out.
- To keep your account secure and resolve support requests.
- To diagnose crashes and improve stability (diagnostics only).

## Data sharing
We do **not** sell your data and do **not** share it with third parties for
advertising. We do not display ads. Diagnostic data is processed by Google Firebase
solely as our service provider.

## Security
Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) between the app and the Fineract backend.

## Your choices
- You can request deletion of your account and associated data through your group
  organizer or via the support contact below.
- You can operate the app offline; sync happens only when connectivity is available.

## Children
MifosSave is intended for adults (18+) managing community savings groups and is not
directed at children.
