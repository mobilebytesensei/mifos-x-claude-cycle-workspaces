# MifosSave — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-08 · Publisher: Mifos Initiative_

MifosSave is open-source community-banking software that helps savings groups (VSLAs,
ROSCAs, SACCOs, SHGs and similar) keep their meeting, savings, loan and share-out
records. This policy explains what the app collects and why.

## What we collect
- **Contact info** — your name and your phone number or email address, used to create
  your account and identify you within your groups.
- **Financial info & user content** — the savings, contributions, loans, repayments,
  fines and balances you and your group record. This data belongs to your group.
- **Usage & diagnostics** — anonymous product-interaction events and crash data (via
  Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics) that help us improve reliability.

## Storage and sync
MifosSave is offline-first. Data is stored on your device and synced to the Mifos /
Apache Fineract backend operated by your organization. We do not send your group's
records to any other third party.

## Tracking and ads
We do **not** track you across other apps or websites, we do **not** display ads, and
we do **not** sell your data. Diagnostic data is processed by Google Firebase solely
as our service provider.

## Security
Data is encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS).

## Your choices
You can request deletion of your account and data through your group organizer or the
support contact below. You can operate offline; sync occurs only when connected.

## Children
MifosSave is intended for adults (4+ rating; designed for adult group members) and is
not directed at children.
