1 · Choose a demo user & authorize

Select a persona and click Sign in & Authorize. That runs POST /auth/login, grabs the returned sessionToken, and wires it into Swagger's Authorize for you — so every Try it out → Execute below is sent as Authorization: Bearer <sessionToken>.

2 · Also authorize direct-Fineract calls

The sessionToken above only covers companion BFF endpoints — groups/{id}/savings*, groups/{id}/shareout/*, groups/{id}/rotation/* (tagged savings/share-out). Every other endpoint (groups, clients-members, loans, meetings, /datatables/*, /batches, /self/*) calls Fineract directly and needs Fineract's own HTTP Basic Auth — click the button below to wire it in one step (dev creds for mifos-bank-2, per idea-layer/ARCHITECTURE.md §3). Do both 1 and 2 once; every endpoint on this page is then live.
