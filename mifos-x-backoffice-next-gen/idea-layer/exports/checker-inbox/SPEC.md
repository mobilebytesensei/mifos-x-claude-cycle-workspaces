## Capabilities

- **has_ui** — Compose list screen: entity/action filter chips over a queue of pending maker-checker commands with inline per-row decision controls.
- **has_offline_cache** — the queue reads CACHE_FIRST_SWR through Store5 and browses fully offline; decisions queue in the durable outbox.
