## Capabilities

### has_ui
Compose sectioned form — Details · Terms · Settings · Charges · Accounting — every field, bound and option rendered from the merged Fineract product + template.

### has_offline_cache
Product and template reads are CACHE_FIRST_SWR through Store5 (`loan_product_cache`, `loan_product_template_cache`); the form opens and stays editable/draftable offline.

### has_flow
Drilled in from the M08 products & charges list (edit) or the "new product" affordance (create); `loading → content/empty/error`, Save enqueues the outbox write.

### requires_auth
Reads and save run under the authenticated Fineract session; gated on `READ_LOANPRODUCT` for load and `CREATE_LOANPRODUCT`/`UPDATE_LOANPRODUCT` for edit.
