## Screen Intent

The teller/cash-drawer module: an office-scoped teller list drilling into a teller's cashiers + cash position, with cashier assignment and the cash-movement lifecycle — **allocate cash to cashier**, **settle cash from cashier**, and view the teller transaction ledger. Cash-movement commands queue to the offline outbox (optimistic, reconcile-on-sync); a persistent offline banner shows queued-movement counts; rejected commands surface in `needs-attention-inbox`.

## App Shell (resolved from `app-shell.yaml`)

- **Top app bar** — small M3, `surface`, title "Tellers & Cash", menu/back leading.
- **Bottom navigation** — visible as a module root. **FAB** — `new_teller_button` → `dynamic-template-forms` (CREATE_TELLER).
- **Offline banner** — `offline_banner` shows cached cash figures + "N queued · will sync when online".

### State: `loading` — teller/cashier shimmer
Header + date filter stay; teller list / cashier rows render as `shimmer-skeleton`.

### State: `empty` — no tellers
No tellers in office scope → "New teller" (CREATE_TELLER) or a plain "No tellers in your office scope".

### State: `submitting` — cash movement queuing
An allocate/settle/assign command posts or writes to the outbox; the control spins with a "Queued" chip; cash figures update optimistically and reconcile on sync.

### State: `error` — fetch/command failed
Server error + Retry; `review_failed_button` routes rejected cash movements to `needs-attention-inbox`.

### State: `no-network` — offline cached
Persistent offline banner; cached teller/cash figures served, queued movements counted, captive-portal aware.

- All cash balances in Roboto Mono for aligned drawer reconciliation.
