## Screen Intent

The unified "what needs me" feed — a fan-in aggregation across four permission-filtered sources (maker-checker approvals, failed offline replays, overdue collections/loan tasks, unread alerts), grouped by category with per-group counts and inline triage. Each item deep-links into its owning module (M15/M06/M04/M17). Failed-sync rows expose **Retry / Discard** (with an explicit ConflictStrategy); approval rows expose inline **Approve / Reject** (only when `canCheck` or `CHECKER_SUPER_USER`); alert/overdue rows expose **Snooze**. A per-category error strip retries just its failed source without disturbing already-loaded groups — a single source failure never blanks the inbox. This is the role-aware home surface reachable as a primary destination, and the sink referenced by every module's "review failed" affordance and by `offline-sync-engine` after a rejected replay.

## Accessibility & Density Notes

- Per-item actions are context-typed (Retry/Discard on failed-sync rows, Approve on `canCheck` approval rows, Snooze on alert/overdue rows) — no irrelevant action is ever shown on a row.
- `nai_discard` is destructive and requires a confirm dialog with an explicit ConflictStrategy choice — never a silent delete (per docs.yaml acceptance ref).
- Per-category error strip (`nai_group_retry`) retries just its own source — one failed source never blanks the other loaded groups nor forces the whole-screen `error` state.
- Priority is conveyed by badge **text** ("Critical"/"High"/"Normal") plus colour, never colour alone (WCAG 1.4.1).
- All interactive rows/buttons declare `accessibility_label` (e.g. "Open this item in its owning module", "Retry this failed command") for screen-reader parity with the visual label.
- Minimum touch target 48dp (`touch_targets.comfortable`) on all row-level action buttons.
