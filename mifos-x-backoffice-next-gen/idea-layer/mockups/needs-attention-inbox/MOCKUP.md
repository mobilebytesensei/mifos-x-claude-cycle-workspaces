## Screen Intent

The unified "what needs me" feed — a fan-in aggregation across sources (approvals, failed syncs, overdue tasks, alerts), grouped by category with per-group counts and inline triage. Each item deep-links into its owning module. Failed-sync rows expose **Retry / Discard** (with ConflictStrategy); approval rows expose inline **Approve** (only when `canCheck`); alert/overdue rows expose **Snooze**. A per-category error strip retries just its failed source without disturbing loaded groups. This is the sink referenced by every module's "review failed" affordance.

### State: `empty` — nothing needs you
"All clear" panel with an affordance to the M17 sync console (`nai_empty_sync` → `m17-sync-settings`).

## Accessibility & Density Notes

- Per-item actions are context-typed (Retry/Discard on failed syncs, Approve on canCheck approvals, Snooze on alerts) — no irrelevant action shown.
- Discard is destructive + confirm with an explicit ConflictStrategy choice.
- Per-category error strip retries just its source — one failed source never blanks loaded groups.
- Priority conveyed by badge text + colour, not colour alone.
