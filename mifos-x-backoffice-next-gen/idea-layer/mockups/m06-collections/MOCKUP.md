## Screen Intent

The field-operations collections module: pick a center/group + meeting date, generate the collection sheet (CACHE_FIRST_SWR so a 30-member sheet renders under 1s), capture the amount-collected per client row (loan repayment due + savings deposit due), watch the running captured-vs-expected total, and **submit the whole sheet as one atomic batch** (`POST /v1/batches` enclosingTransaction) or enqueue it to the durable outbox while offline for exactly-once replay. Rejected rows surface in `needs-attention-inbox`.

### State: `loading` — sheet shimmer
Header + context stay; grouped client rows render as `shimmer-skeleton` while the sheet generates (or serves cached).

### State: `empty` — nothing due
No outstanding dues for the selected meeting → "nothing due for this meeting" with an option to pick another center/group/date.

### State: `no-network` — offline cached
Persistent "offline · cached sheet" banner; capture continues, submit enqueues for replay.

### State: `submitting` — atomic batch in flight
The captured sheet posts as one atomic `POST /v1/batches` (or writes to the outbox); progress indicator + disabled sheet prevent double taps.

### State: `queued-offline` — enqueued
"Queued · will sync when online" confirmation; the sheet replays exactly-once (durable idempotency key); any rejected row surfaces in `needs-attention-inbox`.

## Accessibility & Density Notes

- Submit is one atomic batch — a partial network drop never leaves a half-collected sheet.
- Capture fields numeric-keyboard, prefilled with the due; running captured-vs-expected total surfaces mistakes.
- Amounts in Roboto Mono keep columns aligned at meeting close.
- Rejected rows surface in `needs-attention-inbox`, never silently dropped.
