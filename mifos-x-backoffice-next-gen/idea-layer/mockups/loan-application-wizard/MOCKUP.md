## Screen Intent

The multi-step new-loan wizard drilled into from the M04 loan list. Renders a step form from `GET /v1/loans/template` (products, min/max principal, charge and collateral options), previews the amortised schedule via `?command=calculateLoanSchedule` before submit, validates each step, and submits `POST /v1/loans` through the offline outbox with a durable idempotency key. On success it lands on the new loan's detail. Steps: **Product & terms → Charges/collateral → Schedule preview → Review**. `requires_permission: [CREATE_LOAN]`.

## App Shell (resolved from `app-shell.yaml`)

- **Top app bar** — small M3, `surface`, title "New Loan" + client subtitle, back leading (drill-in from loan list).
- **Bottom navigation** — suppressed on this wizard flow.
- **FAB** — none; navigation is the stepper's Back/Next + final Submit. **Offline banner** — draftable offline; a queued Submit shows an outbox badge.

## Accessibility & Density Notes

- Next is enabled only when the current step passes template validation (min/max principal, allowed term, required charges).
- Schedule preview lets the officer verify installments/interest/fees before an irreversible submit.
- Submit enqueues to the outbox exactly-once — a signal drop mid-submit never double-books a loan.
- Money + rate figures in Roboto Mono for legible term review.
