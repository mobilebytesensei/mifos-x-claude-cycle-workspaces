## Screen Intent

The searchable, paged, office-scoped client roster drilled into from the M01 dashboard "active clients" tile and "new client" quick action. Loads `GET /v1/clients` (office-scoped, offset/limit paged) into rows (display name, account no, status chip, office, assigned staff) under a search box (escalates to `GET /v1/search?resource=clients`) and status/office filter chips. Each row drills into `client-detail-360`. A permission-gated **New client** FAB opens the dynamic create form (`GET /v1/clients/template` → `POST /v1/clients` through the offline outbox). Reads cache-first through Store5 with paging — fully browsable offline; clients created offline appear immediately. `requires_permission: [READ_CLIENT]`.

## Accessibility & Density Notes

- FAB carries "Start a new client application" a11y label; hidden entirely (not disabled) without CREATE_CLIENT.
- Search enforces min length (ignores single-char noise before hitting `/v1/search`) and max length (Fineract bound).
- Status chips carry text; account numbers in Roboto Mono for aligned scanning down the roster.
- Offline-created rows are visibly flagged (Queued) so an officer knows the client isn't yet server-confirmed.
