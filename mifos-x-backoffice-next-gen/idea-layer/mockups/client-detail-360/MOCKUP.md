## Screen Intent

The 360° client profile drilled into from the M02 clients list (`open_client`). Loads `GET /v1/clients/{clientId}?associations=all` + `GET /v1/clients/{clientId}/accounts` into an identity/KYC header (display name, account no, external id, status chip, office, assigned staff, legal form) over a tabbed body — **General** (address, family, identifiers/datatables) · **Accounts** (loans, savings, shares with balances) · **Notes/Documents**. A permission-gated action bar exposes client-lifecycle commands (`activate` / `assignStaff` / …). Each account row drills into its own detail. Reads cache-first through Store5 (`ClientDetailStore`); mutations enqueue to the offline outbox with a durable key. `requires_permission: [READ_CLIENT]`.

## App Shell (resolved from `app-shell.yaml`)

- **Top app bar** — small M3, `surface`, title = client display name, back leading (drill-in from client-list).
- **Bottom navigation** — suppressed on this detail.
- **FAB** — none (lifecycle actions live in the gated action bar). **Offline banner** — a stale cached client shows a "background-refreshing" badge rather than a top banner.

## Accessibility & Density Notes

- Status chip carries text ("Active" / "Pending" / "Closed"), never colour alone.
- Lifecycle buttons are permission- AND status-gated; the domain use case re-checks `canDo` before dispatch/replay.
- Account balances in Roboto Mono keep the accounts tab scannable as a mini-ledger.
- `cd_account_row` ≥ 48dp touch target with a "Open this account's detail" a11y label.
