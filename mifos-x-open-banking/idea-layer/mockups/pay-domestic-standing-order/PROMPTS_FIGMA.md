## 8. Animation / Motion

- **Intensity**: low (`design_read.dials.motion = 2`)
- **Reduce motion**: honoured; all transitions are instant when enabled
- Step transitions: cross-fade (150ms, `motion.durations.short`)
- Dropdown menu open: slide + fade (150ms)
- Button press: ripple only, no scale (low-motion dial)
- Loading shimmer: shimmer sweep 1.5s loop, `motion.emphasis_easing`
- iOS equivalent: `CABasicAnimation` with `timingFunction: kCAMediaTimingFunctionEaseInEaseOut`
- Android equivalent: `Interpolator: FastOutSlowInInterpolator`
