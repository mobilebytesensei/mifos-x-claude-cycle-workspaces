Privacy Policy

verified_user

This policy complies with UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018, and EU GDPR (Regulation 2016/679).

Data We Collect

The app handles only what it needs: (a) Access tokens — issued by your bank after you authorise there, never a password; the app has no password field; (b) Banking data — accounts, balances, transactions, beneficiaries, standing orders, scheduled payments, direct debits and statements, read only where the consent you approved permits it; (c) Payment instructions you compose, sent to your bank to execute; (d) App preferences — theme and notification choices made on this device. The app never requests your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or any other device data.

Lawful Basis for Processing

We process your personal data under the following GDPR lawful bases: (a) Contract performance, processing necessary to provide the banking services you have requested; (b) Legal obligation, AML/KYC checks, regulatory reporting to the FCA; (c) Legitimate interests, fraud detection, security monitoring, crash analytics, balanced against your rights; (d) Consent, for optional marketing communications, which you may withdraw at any time via Settings.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to: provide account balance and transaction display; initiate and record payment transactions; manage standing orders and direct debits; facilitate KYC onboarding for field officers; detect and prevent fraudulent activity; deliver in-app push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging, Android only); and generate aggregated, anonymised usage reports to improve app performance.

Third-Party Data Sharing

Data leaves this app in exactly one direction: API requests to your bank's Open Banking endpoint, over mutual TLS. HSBC processes those requests under its own privacy notice. The app shares nothing with advertisers, data brokers or social networks, and it embeds no third-party tracking SDKs.

Data Retention

Your banking data lives at your bank and follows its retention schedule — this app is a window onto it, never its custodian. The local cache and your tokens are cleared when you sign out or revoke the consent, and uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on this device. Under UK Open Banking rules an account-access consent must be reconfirmed every 90 days, so access lapses on its own if you do nothing. Crash reports, where enabled, are kept only as long as needed to diagnose the defect they describe.

Your Rights Under GDPR

Under UK GDPR and EU GDPR you have the right to: (a) Access, request a copy of personal data we hold about you; (b) Rectification, correct inaccurate or incomplete data; (c) Erasure (Right to be Forgotten), request deletion of data where no legal retention obligation applies; (d) Data Portability, receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (JSON); (e) Restriction of Processing, pause processing while accuracy is disputed; (f) Objection, object to processing based on legitimate interests; (g) Withdraw Consent, for any processing based solely on consent. Submit requests to privacy@mifos.org. We will respond within 30 days.

Data Controller & DPO Contact

Data Controller: Mifos Initiative, 1 World Trade Center, New York, NY 10007, USA (operating internationally under applicable data protection regimes).

Your bank: HSBC is the controller for your account and payment data. Concerns about how it processes that data should be raised with HSBC directly, under its own privacy notice.

Data Protection Officer: privacy@mifos.org. To exercise your rights or raise a concern, email our DPO. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk, or with your local supervisory authority in the EU.
